Kvixa

        

          

            
Built with study friends

            
Self-service kiosks and online ordering for grill bars, cafés and takeaway places. I co-developed it together with study friends.

          

          

            
One counter for everything

            
Guests order at the kiosk in the shop or on the restaurant's own website, with one menu behind both. Every order lands on one counter screen, and pickup numbers show on an ordinary TV.

          

          

            
Launched in a real restaurant

            
I helped launch it at Chicken N Chicken Waves in Greve, where the orders now come in through Kvixa every day.

The rest of the shelf

    

    

      

        
AAU CPH Events
2026

        
Second semester project · group leader of six · graded 12

        
A web prototype where students find, filter, save and sign up for events, and organisers manage them. Built on a survey of 94 people and interviews, tested in two rounds of usability tests, with 29 automated tests. I worked on the database, sign up and login, and the event features.

        
GitHub · Node.js · Express · MongoDB · Figma

      

      

        
P1: Social events at AAU CPH
2025

        
First semester project · group of seven · graded 10

        
A program in C that gives an overview of events for students at AAU Copenhagen. It reads events from a text file into structs, sorts them by time and price with qsort, filters by category and alcohol, searches by title and lets you add new events.

        
C · Structs · qsort · File input

      

      

        
Superliga SQL database
2025

        
High school project in Informatik C · group of two · graded 12

        
A relational database of Danish Superliga data with nine tables, an ER diagram and normalisation to third normal form, plus SQL queries with joins, grouping and aggregation.

        
GitHub · SQL · ER diagram · 3NF
